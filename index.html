The imperative

Why this matters now

Three forces are converging to create both an unprecedented opportunity and a closing window for action.

Patients now expect seamless, digital-first experiences across their entire healthcare journey—not just at the point of care.

The consumer healthcare experience has fundamentally shifted. Patients research conditions online, compare providers and suppliers, and demand transparency. The bar has been set by companies outside healthcare, and patients bring those expectations into every interaction with the sleep health ecosystem.

Wearables, GLP-1s, and big tech are expanding the addressable market—but also reshaping who controls the consumer relationship.

Apple, Samsung, and ŌURA are putting sleep health data on millions of wrists. These are structural tailwinds that expand the total market. But they also mean consumers are forming relationships with technology brands before they ever reach ResMed.

Early movers in consumer health orchestration will define the category. Fast followers will struggle to differentiate.

Companies that build direct consumer relationships now gain richer data, stronger brand affinity, and compounding engagement benefits. Waiting means competing in a more crowded and expensive landscape.

Measuring success

How do you know if it’s working?

A successful omnichannel strategy requires clear measures across the patient journey—not just at the point of sale.

01
Demand generation
How many consumers enter the sleep health ecosystem through ResMed-influenced channels?

Measure the quality of new demand and how it moves through awareness, screening, and diagnosis.

02
Conversion
What percentage of patients move from awareness to therapy initiation and long-term adherence?

Track drop-off points across the journey and learn where intervention matters most.

03
Patient engagement
How meaningfully do patients interact with ResMed platforms throughout their journey?

Engagement is a leading indicator of adherence and long-term outcomes.

04
Partner health
Are B2B relationships strengthening as direct consumer engagement grows?

Direct consumer engagement must improve the ecosystem, not create friction for physicians or DMEs.
